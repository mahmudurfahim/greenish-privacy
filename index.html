Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026

Greenish Trade Ltd. ("Greenish Trade", "we", "us") runs an online store that sells products shipped directly from third-party suppliers. We collect only what we need to take your order, deliver it and support you. We do not sell your personal information.

This policy explains what personal information we collect when you visit our website, place an order or contact us, and how we handle it. By using our website you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information we collect

Information you give us:

  • Name, email address and phone number
  • Shipping and billing addresses
  • Order details and messages you send to our support team
  • Account details, if you create an account

Information collected automatically:

  • IP address, browser type, device type and operating system
  • Pages viewed, links clicked, referring website and time spent on pages
  • Approximate location based on your IP address

Payment information: card and wallet details are entered directly with our payment processors. We never see or store your full card number.

3. Who we share it with

Because we operate a dropshipping model, your order details are passed to the suppliers who pack and ship your items. We share information only with:

  • Suppliers and fulfilment partners: your name, shipping address, phone number and the items ordered, so they can deliver your parcel.
  • Shipping carriers: to deliver and track your order.
  • Payment processors: to take payment and prevent fraud.
  • Service providers: website hosting, email delivery, analytics and customer support tools that work on our behalf.
  • Authorities: when required by law or to protect our rights and customers.

We do not sell your personal information. If our business is sold or merged, your information may transfer to the new owner, who must honour this policy.

4. Cookies and similar technologies

We use cookies to keep items in your cart, remember your preferences, measure site traffic and, where permitted, show relevant advertising. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, though parts of the store may not work properly without them.

5. International transfers

Our suppliers, carriers and service providers may be located in other countries, so your information may be processed outside your country of residence. Where required, we use appropriate safeguards such as contractual protections to keep your information protected.

6. How long we keep your information

We keep order and account information for as long as needed to provide our services and to meet tax, accounting and legal requirements, typically up to six years after your last order. Marketing data is kept until you unsubscribe. After that, we delete or anonymise it.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Correct information that is inaccurate
  • Request deletion of your information
  • Object to or restrict certain processing
  • Receive a copy of your data in a portable format
  • Withdraw consent for marketing at any time
  • Complain to your local data protection authority

To use any of these rights, email us at the address in the contact section. We may need to verify your identity first and will respond within 30 days.

8. Security

We use encryption (HTTPS), access controls and trusted providers to protect your information. No online system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security, but we act promptly if a problem is found.

9. Children

Our store is not intended for anyone under 16. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top shows when it last changed. Significant changes will be announced on our website or by email.
